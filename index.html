Bubble Plume Data Repository

A unified platform for sourcing raw and processed bubble plume data from key publications.

This repository collects, organizes, and provides access to bubble plume datasets extracted from selected publications. The goal is to make raw and processed data easier to find, download, reproduce, and compare.

Data catalog

Each record provides the paper DOI and two data download options. CSV files are the primary machine-readable data files; XLSX files are formatted companion workbooks for easier inspection.

Milgram, J. H. (1983). Mean flow in round bubble plumes. Journal of Fluid Mechanics, 133, 345–376. Tabular data DOI CSV XLSX Compiled mean-flow dataset from Milgram’s analysis of several experimental sources, including Milgram & Van Houten (1982), Fannelop & Sjoen (1980), and larger-scale Bugg Spring measurements. The data include radial velocity-profile information reduced to plume width, centerline velocity, flux, gamma, and alpha where available. Use source_table and source_study to identify each data block.
Li, G., Wang, B., Wu, H., & DiMarco, S. F. (2020). Impact of bubble size on the integral characteristics of bubble plumes in quiescent and unstratified water. International Journal of Multiphase Flow, 125, 103230. Nondimensionalized comparison data DOI CSV XLSX Experiments in quiescent, unstratified water comparing air-stone and single-orifice bubble plumes. The Li et al. 2020 data include nondimensionalized plume half-width, centerline velocity, volume flux, and momentum flux; some literature-comparison series are also taken from the nondimensionalized plots in Li et al. (2020). Air-stone and single-orifice cases are stored as separate paired series.
Wang, B., Lai, C. C. K., & Socolofsky, S. A. (2019). Mean velocity, spreading and entrainment characteristics of weak bubble plumes in unstratified and stationary water. Journal of Fluid Mechanics, 874, 102–130. Nondimensionalized weak bubble plume data DOI CSV XLSX Large-scale weak bubble-plume experiments in unstratified, stationary water. The plume was generated from a 0.4 mm nozzle in the OTRC basin/pit, with Q1 = 0.25 NL/min and Q2 = 1.2 NL/min measured at five heights from 0.5 to 16 m. The dataset includes normalized mean velocity, plume half-width, apparent entrainment coefficient, volume flux, and momentum flux.
Kobus, H. E. Analysis of the flow induced by air-bubble systems. Coastal Engineering Proceedings, Chapter 65. Single-orifice mean-flow data DOI CSV XLSX Early air-bubble-system study measuring vertical velocities above air discharging into water. The current repository file includes only the single-orifice data: centerline velocity, radial velocity profiles, and volume-flux ratio. Row-of-orifices data are not included in this first version.
Seol, D.-G., Bhaumik, T., Bergmann, C., & Socolofsky, S. A. (2007). Particle Image Velocimetry Measurements of the Mean Flow Characteristics in a Bubble Plume. Journal of Engineering Mechanics, 133(6), 665–676. Alpha / entrainment-coefficient data DOI CSV XLSX PIV/PTV bubble-plume study in quiescent, unstratified ambient water. The current repository file includes only entrainment-coefficient alpha data from selected figures. The alpha data include variation with nondimensional height, bubble Froude number, and dimensionless slip-velocity parameter.
Mingotti, N., & Woods, A. W. (2023). Experiments on fluid entrainment and slip in continuous bubble plumes. Journal of Fluid Mechanics, 973, A22. Slip-plume mean-flow and volume-flux scaling data DOI CSV XLSX Continuous bubble-plume experiments focused on the slip-plume regime, where bubble motion and liquid motion remain strongly separated. The dataset includes bubble-radius spreading coefficient, liquid-to-bubble radius ratio, liquid-to-bubble velocity ratio, and the slip-plume volume-flux coefficient lambda. Lambda is a slip-plume volume-flux coefficient, not the classical entrainment coefficient alpha.
Fraga, B., Stoesser, T., Lai, C. C. K., & Socolofsky, S. A. (2016). A LES-based Eulerian–Lagrangian approach to predict the dynamics of bubble plumes. Ocean Modelling, 97, 27–36. LES-based bubble plume scaling data DOI CSV XLSX LES-based Eulerian–Lagrangian computational bubble-plume dataset validated against ADV/PIV measurements. The reconstructed data include plume radius, entrainment velocity, mass/volume flux, and centerline velocity scaling. Series are grouped by bubble diameter, Dp = 1, 2, and 4 mm.

Notes

  • CSV files are the primary machine-readable data files.
  • XLSX files are formatted companion workbooks with a Details sheet and a Data sheet.
  • Older split CSV files are retained only as source/development files and are not the main public downloads.
  • Future datasets should follow the same structure: one CSV and one formatted XLSX per paper.
